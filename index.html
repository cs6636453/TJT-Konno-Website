Wasabi Card

In the city of Limaru, the pancard serves as the standard integrated circuit (IC) card, providing residents and visitors with seamless access to its comprehensive travel network. Meanwhile, the neighboring TJT authority has issued its own specific smart card, known as the Wasabi card. Despite the different branding, both cards are built upon the exact same IC chip technology. This shared foundation ensures complete interoperability, allowing the Wasabi card to function flawlessly across all systems that accept the pancard, offering travelers a unified and convenient experience across both regions.

TJT B41 Train

We are proud to introduce the all-new B41 train, the latest advancement in Limaru's public transit, built by the renowned Konno Heavy Industries in Chitose. As the most modern train in our fleet, the B41 is equipped with the newest technology, including comprehensive CCTV coverage, a crystal-clear speaker system, and dynamic in-car displays for passenger information. This new series will serve the Kotoha Line, connecting Shinten to Sumiyoshi and stopping at major stations such as Twyford, Mizuno Filius, Chitose, and Kimitsu. During peak hours, a special "Yamabiko" limited-stop service will also be available, serving Mizuna, Gojo, and Katsuragi. Furthermore, the B41's exceptional design has led to its adoption internationally, with an export variant, the B43, now serving the Island Loop Line for the YJJ City MRT.

TJT Bus Services

In addition to its rail network, TJT operates a comprehensive and modern fleet of bus services, primarily based in the city of Mizuno. A key feature of this network is the premium Emmaru BRT (Bus Rapid Transit), which includes express routes BRT-2 and BRT-3, efficiently connecting Mizuno and Katsuragi via Iwanoyuki. For travelers, TJT also offers a convenient direct bus service between Lipan Airport and the Mizuno Pater district.

Reflecting a commitment to sustainability and passenger comfort, our entire bus fleet is powered by clean hydrogen technology and equipped with modern amenities such as in-car passenger information displays and automated announcements. For seamless travel, all buses accept payments from any standard IC card.

Plazma GO!

Beyond its core transportation services, TJT (also known as TJT KDR) has diversified its portfolio by operating a chain of convenience stores. These outlets, formerly known as KONNO 24, are currently undergoing a strategic rebranding. They will now operate under the new name Plazma GO!, following a partnership with or acquisition by the Plazma Hugo Trust. This transition affects all minimarket locations throughout the Limaru region.
