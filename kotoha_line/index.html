TJT B41 Train

We are proud to introduce the all-new B41 train, the latest advancement in Limaru's public transit, built by the renowned Konno Heavy Industries in Chitose. As the most modern train in our fleet, the B41 is equipped with the newest technology, including comprehensive CCTV coverage, a crystal-clear speaker system, and dynamic in-car displays for passenger information. This new series will serve the Kotoha Line, connecting Shinten to Sumiyoshi and stopping at major stations such as Twyford, Mizuno Filius, Chitose, and Kimitsu. During peak hours, a special "Yamabiko" limited-stop service will also be available, serving Mizuna, Gojo, and Katsuragi. Furthermore, the B41's exceptional design has led to its adoption internationally, with an export variant, the B43, now serving the Island Loop Line for the YJJ City MRT.

Konno University

Konno University, the planned fourth institution in the Limaru City Server, is currently under construction in the eastern area of Takarazaki, intended to serve as both an academic center and a social hangout for teenagers. Visually, the campus features distinctive architectural elements, including a prominent building with sleek teal-colored paneling contrasting with a more traditional light brown facade, alongside other modern structures with flat grey roofs and large windows, all set against the backdrop of Limaru's developing urban landscape, complete with adjacent rail lines and carefully laid out roadways. This university is uniquely tailored to the roleplay Minecraft environment, with a curriculum focused on specialized courses such as Redstone Engineering, Command Block Theory, and Train Carts Engineering, ensuring it develops the technical talent necessary for the continued growth and operation of Limaru's complex infrastructure.

Lake Kimitsuko

Lake Kimitsu, or Kimitsuko, is a small, scenic lake located near the Limaru City Server districts of Kimitsu and Chitose, serving as a much-needed tranquil retreat that provides a beautiful, natural scenery view and is considered the best relax place to visit, featuring rolling, forested terrain and clear water that contrasts sharply with the nearby urban development, yet remains accessible due to the way the Limaru railway infrastructure (operated by TJT) often integrates seamlessly into the surrounding natural environment.

Chitose Town

A key district in Limaru, Chitose Town has evolved from a quiet farming community into a pioneering smart city. Its transformation is driven by the integration of cutting-edge AI and drone technology, serving as the central headquarters for both the Bureau of Mizuno Land Transport and the Smart City Traffic Department. As a vital center for transit innovation, the town also includes the official TJT driver training facility for train and bus operators and functions as the primary test site for Limaru's fleet of autonomous AI buses, cementing its status as a forward-thinking technological hub.

Mizuno City

Located in the northeastern region of Limaru, Mizuno City is a highly developed urban center renowned for its unique architecture. It serves as a primary hub for the testing and implementation of advanced AI systems and other modern smart technologies. The intricate details and diverse corners of the city are uniquely showcased within the server's "Buses" articles, allowing visitors to discover its landmarks by exploring the various routes that navigate its streets.

Otaki, Hokuyo & Mizuna

Otaki is a uniquely charming district celebrated for its classic Western-style architecture and historic village atmosphere. Characterized by its numerous winding canals and intricate, old-world buildings, the area is widely regarded as one of the most scenic and best places to visit in Limaru. This picturesque destination is conveniently accessible, with visitors able to arrive via the nearby Hokuyo and Mizuna stations.

Toyosato & Maruyama

Toyosato is a newly developed city emerging in the region southeast of Limaru, representing a fresh chapter of expansion and growth for the server. The area's most defining and picturesque feature is the prominent Maruyama, a large mountain that commands the local landscape. The main railway creates a memorable journey for passengers as it does not tunnel through the mountain but instead circles its entire base, offering an extended and scenic detour with constantly shifting views of the surrounding terrain. While local transit options within Toyosato itself are still under development, reflecting its recent establishment, the city is strategically planned to become a critical node in the server's transportation network. Upon completion of its infrastructure, Toyosato is slated to host a major train hub, connecting the southeastern territories and promising to become a bustling center for future travel and commerce.
