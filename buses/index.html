TJT Bus Services

In addition to its rail network, TJT operates a comprehensive and modern fleet of bus services, primarily based in the city of Mizuno. A key feature of this network is the premium Emmaru BRT (Bus Rapid Transit), which includes express routes BRT-2 and BRT-3, efficiently connecting Mizuno and Katsuragi via Iwanoyuki. For travelers, TJT also offers a convenient direct bus service between Lipan Airport and the Mizuno Pater district.

Reflecting a commitment to sustainability and passenger comfort, our entire bus fleet is powered by clean hydrogen technology and equipped with modern amenities such as in-car passenger information displays and automated announcements. For seamless travel, all buses accept payments from any standard IC card.

Filius Scout Camp

Nestled in a rustic, natural setting, Filius Adventure Camp, also commonly known as the Filius Scout Camp, offers a premier destination for outdoor enthusiasts of all kinds. Designed to serve both organized scout troops and casual campers seeking a relaxing hangout, the camp provides a perfect blend of structured activities and leisurely recreation. Visitors will find a dedicated tent area for resting and a central meeting point perfect for socializing, group activities, or simply chilling by the campfire. The camp boasts a wide array of exciting pursuits, inviting adventurers to go fishing in serene waters, embark on scenic boating excursions down the river, experience the thrill of the zipline course, and test their aim at the archery range. With these and many other outdoor challenges available, Filius Adventure Camp provides a comprehensive and memorable wilderness experience for all who visit.

The Garden of Goats

The Garden of Goats, while not a conventional attraction, holds a unique and solemn purpose. Functioning as a graveyard, the site is thoughtfully designed with park-like scenery and is situated near the main railway, offering a space for quiet reflection amidst a peaceful environment for those who visit.

Fryingpan Lane

Tucked away within the bustling metropolis of Mizuno, Fryingpan Lane is a small yet vibrant commercial hub offering a more intimate shopping experience. While not a large district, the lane is densely packed with a variety of stores, anchored by the prominent and modern Plazma GO! Building. Alongside this major retailer, visitors can explore a charming selection of other local shops and businesses. For those looking for a refreshment, local rumor says that the Saiba Cafe, also located on this street, serves some of the best-tasting treats in the city, making it a must-visit spot while exploring the area.

Filius Theatre

The Filius Theatre and the adjacent Qingque Commercial District together form a unique destination where grand entertainment meets traditional charm. The centerpiece of the area is the magnificent Filius Theater, a large, contemporary venue that stands as a premier location for plays, concerts, and other major cultural events, its impressive facade glowing brightly against the night sky. In direct contrast, the Qingque Commercial District offers a more tranquil and picturesque atmosphere with its beautifully crafted, traditionally styled buildings, serene gardens, and a distinctive water wheel. This thoughtful combination allows visitors to enjoy a spectacular performance at the theater before or after exploring the quaint shops and relaxing scenery of the commercial area, creating a culturally rich and diverse experience.

Midori Food Market

In the Filius & Pater sub-districts of Mizuno, a striking architectural landmark defines the local landscape, featuring two impressive buildings linked across a tranquil river. These structures are the verdant Midori Food Market and the distinct, earth-toned Momoi Time Center. The two are joined by an elegant connecting bridge, which is famously rumored to have a glass floor that offers pedestrians a thrilling view of the water flowing below. While these twin establishments may not be sprawling commercial hubs, they provide a specialized and unique experience for visitors. Together, the market, the center, and their remarkable bridge create a memorable and picturesque sight, serving as a key point of interest in the district.

Minseo Park

Minseo Park stands as the verdant heart of Mizuno City, centrally located within the bustling Mizuno Pater district, offering a vital green space amidst the urban landscape. This beautifully landscaped park provides a welcoming oasis for residents and visitors alike, serving as an ideal spot for various leisure activities. It is a popular destination for those seeking to relax and unwind, offering ample opportunities for people to hang out with friends, enjoy a peaceful moment of contemplation, or engage in a range of recreational pursuits, making it a beloved gathering place for the community.

Promenade Road

Promenade Road is the main artery and central hub of Mizuno's bustling business district, defining the commercial heart of the city. This major thoroughfare is flanked on both sides by a diverse array of multi-story buildings, housing a wide range of shops, offices, and other key establishments. Lined with bright streetlights and organized with clear traffic signals and crosswalks, the road is engineered to handle a constant flow of pedestrians and vehicles, reflecting its critical role in the city's economy. As the core of Mizuno's commercial activity, Promenade Road is a dynamic and essential street, constantly alive with the energy of city life and enterprise.

Lunatic Eye

Dominating the Mizuno skyline is a premier entertainment and educational complex featuring two of the city's most captivating attractions: the Lunatic Eye and the Astrex Galaxy. The Lunatic Eye is a colossal Ferris wheel that lifts visitors high above the city, offering breathtaking panoramic views of the sprawling urban landscape below, including the entirety of the Pater and Sanctus Districts. At the base of this towering structure lies the Astrex Galaxy, a modern institution dedicated to cosmic exploration and learning. Here, guests can embark on an educational journey to understand our home planet, the solar system, and the vast wonders of the entire galaxy. This unique pairing of terrestrial and celestial viewpoints makes the complex a must-visit destination, offering a chance to marvel at the scale of both the city and the universe.

Eientei District

Nestled near the city's modern attractions is a tranquil haven for wellness, housed within a beautifully designed, Chinatown-style building. This is the home of the Plain Eastern Asian Medical Clinic, a specialized center offering traditional remedies and treatments for common ailments such as back pain and migraines. The clinic is designed as an authentic compound, featuring classic Eastern architecture with a distinctive gateway, traditional lanterns, and a peaceful inner courtyard that provides a calming atmosphere for patients. Its serene and traditional design offers a harmonious contrast to the surrounding urban environment, standing as a unique institution dedicated to health and well-being.
