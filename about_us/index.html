About us

Takarazaki Joint Transport Inc. (TJT) is a strategic collaboration founded by Konno Pte., Ltd. (technology and transport systems), Plazma Hugo Trust, and Reisen Studio Co., Ltd. (architecture and design), established primarily because Konno's founder, winsanmwtv, has limited time for active management in the Minecraft world. By pooling the specialized expertise of these three companies, TJT ensures the continuity and expansion of reliable, high-quality services—including the Kotoha Line, KTB Buses, Mizuno and Emmaru BRTs, Konno University, various malls and stores, and the Wasabi Card—to all passengers, commuters, and customers across the city of Limaru.

TJT Color Scheme

  • Green signifies our commitment to eco-friendly and sustainable transit operations.
  • Gray represents our focus on smart and technology-driven systems.
  • Sky Blue embodies our dedication to modern and future-focused design and infrastructure.
